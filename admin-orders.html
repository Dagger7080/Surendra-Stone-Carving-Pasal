Download order history

Pulls every order from the Google Sheet log and saves it as one Excel file.

This page is not linked from the storefront and has no real login — anyone with this URL and your passphrase can download every customer's order details. Don't share this link, and don't bookmark it somewhere public.

Full setup steps are in order-log-setup.md. You can also just open the Google Sheet itself and use File → Download → Microsoft Excel.
